A partial history

Before the dark.

Records recovered from the eastern archive. Several dates have been deliberately removed.

Cycle 00

The sun stops.

Every clock in the Meridian loses the same eleven seconds. The daylight remains, but no longer travels.

Cycle 06

The first lighthouse wakes.

A green fire appears above the old sea. It burns without fuel and casts a shadow in the wrong direction.

Cycle 19

The houses divide.

The Court seals its doors. The Saltbound descends underground. The Choir begins singing to an empty sky.

Cycle 47

Someone answers.

The latest transmission contains a second voice beneath the speaker. It knows the archivist by name.
